### 重放

在 F 盘保留的材料目录运行。下列命令先实核发布目录中的一对文件，然后以新文件名输出一轮完整证据。

```powershell
$base = 'F:/DSHA_RESTART'
$ev = "$base/artifacts/build377-local"
$repo = "$base/source/build377-local"
$audit = "$ev/source-rights"
$apk = "$ev/local-release/dsha-0.2.2-rc.5.apk"
$zip = "$ev/local-release/dsha-proot-talloc-source.zip"
$ndk = "$base/toolchains/current/android-sdk/ndk/26.3.11579264"
$before = "$base/release/0.2.2-rc.5/history/0.2.2-rc.5-build376/dsha-0.2.2-rc.5.apk"
& "$audit/python39/python.exe" -B "$repo/tools/proot_source_distribution.py" --apk $apk --check-source $zip
& "$audit/python39/python.exe" -B "$audit/replay-source.py" --source $repo --audit $audit --ndk $ndk --before-apk $before --after-apk $apk --companion $zip --output "$ev/final4/source-rights/replayed.json"
```

`$before` 取实际保留的 build376 历史路径，完整摘要为 `b9ec7b9a45b1c3463b5ea820cd36b9a32d4aa0228f372fee96080714fc3397cc`。交付前该包还在常规正式目录时，可将该参数指回其当时位置。自动从签名调用、签名产物、源码附件三份回执建立本次绑定的重放入口为 [bind-signed-release.py](../../source-rights/bind-signed-release.py)。
